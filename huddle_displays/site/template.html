Architecture

The panel has no clock

Every wake, the panel asks the add-on “what now?” and is told exactly how many seconds to sleep. It never runs SNTP. Three things follow, and they are the reason the design is shaped this way:

  • Alignment is recomputed server-side every cycle, so ESP32 RTC drift of 1–2% never accumulates. A panel is never more than one cycle out of step.
  • No SNTP round trip — about 1.5 s less awake time per cycle, which is real battery on a device that wakes 96 times a day.
  • The schedule, day window, privacy rule and layout all live in one add-on option — not in ten firmware flashes.

The firmware is deliberately dumb: fetch an image, dump it to the panel, ask how long to sleep, sleep. All the thinking is server-side, where iterating takes two seconds in a browser instead of ten OTA flashes.

Do this first

Two Exchange settings decide whether this works at all

Blocker

Exchange creates room mailboxes with DeleteSubject $true and AddOrganizerToSubject $true. The room's copy of every booking has its subject thrown away and replaced with the organiser's name. Your panel would show “Sanjana Taneja” where the meeting title belongs, and no amount of code fixes it — the real subject was never written to the room calendar.

RemovePrivateProperty $false is the second one: it preserves the Private flag that the redaction keys off. Without it, every booking arrives as normal sensitivity and nothing is ever hidden.

Connect-ExchangeOnline

# run for each of the 10 rooms
Set-CalendarProcessing -Identity "boardroom@lwyd.in" `
  -DeleteSubject $false `
  -AddOrganizerToSubject $false `
  -RemovePrivateProperty $false `
  -AddAdditionalResponse $false

These apply to future bookings only. Exchange will not backfill subjects on meetings that already exist — run it first, then let a day of bookings accumulate before judging the output.

Timing

Landing on the boundary, and sleeping through Saturday

The panel wakes 25 seconds before each boundary, so by the time the ~4.5 s e-paper refresh finishes, the clock reads :14, :29, :44 or :59. The “Updated HH:MM” stamp is rendered at request time, so it always tells the truth about the image in front of you.

Weekend sleep is chunked on purpose. One 60-hour deep sleep would drift by up to an hour. Instead the panel takes six-hour naps and is told render: false for all but the last — it wakes, asks, and goes straight back to sleep without touching the e-paper, which is the expensive part of a cycle. Eight extra wakes across a weekend cost about 0.4 mAh. The final nap lands it at Monday 00:13:35, in time for the 00:14 refresh.

Power

Battery stopped being the constraint

Support docks all ten every weekend, so the budget is five working days, not three months. One full cycle costs about 128 µAh. Against a five-day budget, worst-case 800 µA deep-sleep current:

RefreshCycles/dayRuntimeMargin
15 min shipped 96~57 days 11×
5 min288~32 days 6.4×
2 min720~16 days 3.2×
1 min1440~9 days 1.8×

At 15 minutes you're using about 5% of the cell each week — shallow enough that it'll barely age the battery.

A five-minute refresh is comfortably within budget, and it's the biggest felt improvement available: a room booked two minutes ago shows up before anyone walks to it. It's one option, no reflash — the panels ask the server for their schedule. The only cost is twelve visible refresh flashes an hour instead of four. Shipped at 15 because that's what you specified; try 5 for a week.

The fuel gauge is now optional. On a fixed weekly charge you're never guessing at charge state. Cheaper insurance with no soldering: alert on silence — any panel that hasn't hit /plan in 45 minutes on a weekday is flat, crashed or off the network.

Operations

Ten identical panels coming off the wall every Friday

Risk

Over a year that's 520 hangs by people who don't know one unit from another. A panel will end up on the wrong door. If the room were baked into firmware, fixing it would mean a reflash.

So it isn't. Firmware carries the panel id; the door is a line in the add-on options. A swap is a config edit, effective on the next cycle. Two more things make it safe:

  • The room name is the second-largest thing on the screen, so a mis-hang is obvious to the first person who walks past.
  • tools/make_rooms.py writes PANEL-LABELS.txt — print it, stick one on the back of each unit. Support reads the label, not the screen.

One instruction for the support team resolves most of what they'll ever report: if a screen looks wrong or stale, press the reset button on the back. It forces an immediate wake, fetch and redraw.

Panels asleep on the dock still charge — the charge circuit is independent of the MCU. They keep waking on schedule all Monday morning, so a panel re-hung at 09:00 shows data at most 15 minutes old.

Install

Room one, end to end

  1. Run the Exchange cmdlet on all ten rooms

    Above. Nothing downstream is correct until this is done.

  2. Install the add-on

    Copy the folder into /addons/huddle_displays, then Settings → Add-ons → ⋮ → Check for updates → Local add-ons. Drop your logo at /share/huddle/logo.png; without it the header sets a LWYD wordmark.

  3. Generate the ten room files

    python3 tools/make_rooms.py rooms.csv

    Writes esphome/panel-NN.yaml ×10, prints the rooms: block to paste into the add-on options, and writes PANEL-LABELS.txt for the back of each unit. panel_id is the physical unit, room_id is the door — keeping them separate is what makes a wrong-door swap a config edit.

  4. Check the render before touching hardware

    http://homeassistant.local:8099/preview/panel-01

    Live screen in a browser, reloading every 15 s. Iterate on the layout here — restarting the add-on is a two-second loop, reflashing ten panels is not.

  5. Flash panel-01 over USB

    Put huddle-base.yaml beside the per-room files, set wifi_ssid, wifi_password and ota_password in secrets.yaml, and flash. Everything after this is OTA.

Operations

Flashing something that's asleep

A deep-sleeping panel ignores OTA. Hold the window open, flash, then release:

curl -X POST http://homeassistant.local:8099/stay-awake/panel-01?on=1
# at its next wake (≤15 min) the panel calls deep_sleep.prevent
# ... flash it ...
curl -X POST http://homeassistant.local:8099/stay-awake/panel-01?on=0

run_duration: 150s means a wedged cycle sleeps 15 minutes rather than flattening the battery awake — but it also caps the OTA window, so release and re-hold if you need longer.

Still open

Before all ten go on the wall

  • Time a real refresh and tune refresh_settle (default 8 s). It's pure battery — every second of over-estimate costs ~12 µAh a cycle.
  • Try refresh_minutes at 5 minutes for a week — you have the budget, and it's the most noticeable upgrade available.
  • Check booking_url_template resolves for your tenant, or point the QR at whatever booking page you want.
  • Confirm the Graph registration is scoped to the ten room mailboxes.
